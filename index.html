About the doctor

Meet Dr. S. Kaushik Anurag.

Dr. S. Kaushik Anurag, MBBS, has three years of experience and is a Junior Resident in Respiratory Medicine. He offers video consultations for everyday health concerns, diabetes, hypertension, fever, preventive health, and diet and lifestyle guidance. Consultations are available in English, Telugu and Hindi.
