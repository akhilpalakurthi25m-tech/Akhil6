Privacy notice

How website and appointment information is used.

Preview status. This notice covers the current appointment prototype. It must be expanded to cover student accounts, payments, orders and downloads before paid sales begin.

Information currently collected

For appointment scheduling, the website may collect a name, phone number, optional email address, requested appointment date and requested time.

Why it is collected

The information is used to receive, review, confirm, reschedule, cancel and administer appointment requests, and to contact the requester about that appointment.

What not to submit

Do not submit medical reports, prescriptions, detailed symptoms, diagnoses, identification documents or other sensitive medical information through the appointment form.

Storage and access

Appointment information is stored in the website’s secured backend database. Public visitors cannot view appointment records. Access is intended to be limited to the authorised administrator or doctor account.

Retention and requests

Information should be retained only for as long as reasonably necessary. Contact the doctor using the details displayed on the website to request correction or deletion.

Emergency care

This website is not an emergency medical service. In an emergency, contact the appropriate local emergency service or healthcare facility directly.
